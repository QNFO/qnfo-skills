## Positioning summary (single line)
QWAV is a **pre-commercial computing platform** benchmarked in **joules-per-solution (JPCUB)** — p-adic ultrametric architectures explored as an alternative to the qubit-gate-circuit model, with all performance claims published and independently falsifiable.

## Pricing posture (honest)

| Tier | Status | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **JPCUB Benchmark (open)** | LIVE | The benchmark protocol, methodology, and all published results (Zenodo DOIs) | **Free, open** |
| **Research access** | Pre-commercial | QNFO papers, strategy whitepaper, research RSS, dataset requests via Zenodo records | **Free** |
| **Early benchmark partner** | Not yet offered | Third-party platforms measured with JPCUB and published as comparison records | **To be determined** |
| **Enterprise access** | Planned | Access to a deployed QWAV system (none exists yet), SLA, commercial license | **To be determined** |

## Honest limitations (required disclosures)
1. **No deployed production system exists.** Any paid tier would be premature; the page must say so.
2. **Licensing is unresolved.** QNFO content is CC-BY-4.0 / QNFO-ULA / AGPL; the commercial license is explicitly "to be determined".
3. **JPCUB is open and free by design** — the benchmark itself must never be paywalled (falsifiability depends on any party being able to measure any platform).
