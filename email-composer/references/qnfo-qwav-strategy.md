## What QWAV Is NOT

- NOT a "platform as a service" evaluating other hardware
- NOT a measurement tool for the existing quantum industry
- NOT a service layer on IBM/Google/IonQ machines
- NOT a financialized marketing operation

## What QWAV IS

- A company that builds computation using the right physical substrates
- Commercial implementation of the Problem-Substrate Mapping portfolio
- Developer of thermodynamic, optical, neuromorphic, and analog computation systems
- An entity proving honest computation beats narrative-driven computation
- The commercial vehicle for "the substrate IS the algorithm"

## Five Manifesto Principles (Applied Commercially)

1. **Substrate IS the algorithm** → QWAV builds hardware where physics does the computing
2. **Correlation over particle** → QWAV systems measure correlations, not bit-states
3. **JPCUB as universal metric** → Everything competes on joules-per-solution, not marketing
4. **Falsifiability as funding condition** → Products ship with pre-registered success criteria
5. **Independent verification** → Third-party validation, not self-grading
